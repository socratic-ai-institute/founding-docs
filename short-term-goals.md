# Short-Term Goals: Launching the Socratic AI Institute

The Socratic AI Institute's initial phase focuses on three foundational initiatives that will establish our research capabilities, build stakeholder engagement, and demonstrate the practical value of our approach. These short-term goals are designed to create momentum while laying the groundwork for long-term impact.

## Goal 1: Team Formation and Funding Acquisition

**Objective**: Establish a core interdisciplinary team and secure initial funding to support the institute's research and development activities.

### Team Assembly

**Core Research Team**: Recruit 3-5 dedicated researchers with expertise in AI/ML, cognitive science, education, and human-computer interaction to form the institute's intellectual backbone.

**Advisory Board**: Establish a distinguished advisory board including experts in philosophy, education technology, AI ethics, and practical AI implementation to guide strategic direction.

**Student Researchers**: Engage graduate and undergraduate students from UR and other local institutions to provide research support and fresh perspectives.

**Stakeholder Liaisons**: Designate primary contacts from each stakeholder organization to ensure effective coordination and communication.

### Funding Strategy

**Seed Funding**: Secure initial funding of $150,000-$300,000 to support the first year of operations, covering personnel, equipment, and research expenses.

**Grant Opportunities**: Identify and apply for relevant grants from organizations like the National Science Foundation, Department of Education, and private foundations focused on AI research and educational innovation.

**Corporate Partnerships**: Explore partnerships with technology companies interested in human-centered AI development approaches.

**University Support**: Leverage UR Innovation Studio resources and seek additional institutional support for research infrastructure.

**Timeline**: Complete team formation and secure initial funding within 6 months of institute launch.

## Goal 2: Local Academic Research Study

**Objective**: Conduct a rigorous academic study demonstrating the effectiveness of Socratic AI approaches in educational and professional contexts.

### Research Design

**Comparative Study**: Design a controlled study comparing traditional AI-assisted learning with Socratic AI-guided inquiry across multiple domains (e.g., problem-solving, critical thinking, creative ideation).

**Participant Recruitment**: Engage 100-200 participants from UR student body, local professionals, and community members to ensure diverse perspectives and use cases.

**Metrics Development**: Establish clear metrics for measuring the effectiveness of Socratic questioning, including learning outcomes, engagement levels, and long-term retention of skills.

**Ethical Framework**: Develop comprehensive ethical guidelines for human-AI interaction research, ensuring participant privacy and informed consent.

### Implementation Plan

**Phase 1 - Baseline Development**: Create initial Socratic AI prototypes and establish baseline measurements for traditional AI interaction (Months 1-3).

**Phase 2 - Study Execution**: Conduct the comparative study with recruited participants across various scenarios and domains (Months 4-8).

**Phase 3 - Analysis and Publication**: Analyze results, prepare findings for academic publication, and present at relevant conferences (Months 9-12).

### Expected Outcomes

**Academic Publication**: Produce at least one peer-reviewed paper demonstrating the effectiveness of Socratic AI approaches.

**Best Practices**: Develop evidence-based guidelines for implementing Socratic AI in educational and professional settings.

**Proof of Concept**: Create compelling evidence for the value proposition of question-focused AI systems.

## Goal 3: Community Hackathon

**Objective**: Engage the broader Richmond tech community and demonstrate practical applications of Socratic AI through a collaborative hackathon event.

### Event Structure

**Socratic AI Hackathon**: Organize a 48-hour hackathon focused on developing innovative applications of question-focused AI across various domains.

**Challenge Themes**: Establish specific challenge areas such as education, healthcare, business strategy, creative arts, and community problem-solving.

**Expert Mentorship**: Provide teams with access to Socratic AI experts, domain specialists, and technical mentors throughout the event.

**Resource Provision**: Offer participants access to Socratic AI frameworks, APIs, and development tools created by the institute.

### Community Engagement

**Participant Diversity**: Attract 100+ participants including developers, designers, educators, students, and domain experts from across the Richmond area.

**Corporate Involvement**: Engage local businesses to sponsor the event and identify potential real-world applications for winning solutions.

**Academic Integration**: Encourage student participation and consider integrating the hackathon with existing UR programs and courses.

**Media Coverage**: Generate public awareness of Socratic AI concepts through local media coverage and social media engagement.

### Deliverables and Impact

**Working Prototypes**: Generate 15-20 functional prototypes demonstrating various applications of Socratic AI principles.

**Community Network**: Build a network of local developers and innovators interested in human-centered AI development.

**Industry Partnerships**: Identify potential corporate partners interested in implementing Socratic AI solutions.

**Public Awareness**: Raise community awareness about the importance of preserving human agency in AI development.

**Timeline**: Plan and execute the hackathon within 9 months of institute establishment, with 3 months of preparation and follow-up.

## Success Metrics and Milestones

**3-Month Milestone**: Team assembled, initial funding secured, research study design completed.

**6-Month Milestone**: Research study launched, hackathon planning underway, first Socratic AI prototypes operational.

**9-Month Milestone**: Hackathon executed successfully, research study data collection completed.

**12-Month Milestone**: Research findings published, sustainable funding model established, expansion planning initiated.

These short-term goals are designed to establish the Socratic AI Institute as a credible research organization while building the community engagement and practical applications necessary for long-term success. Each goal reinforces the others, creating a comprehensive foundation for advancing human-AI collaboration through intelligent questioning systems.
